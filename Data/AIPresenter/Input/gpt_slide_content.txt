=== SLIDE | 3 | Group Life Insurance Benefits ===
Key details include: Basic Life (BL); Accidental Death, Dismemberment & Disablement Benefit (ADD&D); Total Permanent Disability Benefit (TPDB). Additional details include: Terminal Illness Benefit (TIB); Family Assistance Benefit (FAB).

=== SLIDE | 4 | Who are Eligible? ===
Eligibility for the Group Life Insurance Benefit is extended to all active, regular, and full-time employees aged 18 to 65 who are in good health at the policy's inception. Future full-time employees will be enrolled upon eligibility, and probationary employees will be covered under certain conditions.

=== SLIDE | 6 | ACCIDENTAL DEATH, DISMEMBERMENT, & DISABLEMENT BENEFIT (ADD&D) ===
In the event of an accidental death, dismemberment, or disablement, Manulife will pay the insured employee or their beneficiaries an amount specified in the policy, provided the injury occurs within 180 days of the accident.

=== SLIDE | 8 | ACCIDENTAL DEATH, DISMEMBERMENT, & DISABLEMENT BENEFIT (ADD&D) ===
Accidental dismemberment is defined as the severance of hands or feet at or above the wrist or ankle joints, and total, irrecoverable loss of eyesight.

=== SLIDE | 9 | ACCIDENTAL DEATH, DISMEMBERMENT, & DISABLEMENT BENEFIT (ADD&D) GROUP LIFE INSURANCE ===
EXCLUSIONS on Accidental Death, Dismemberment & Disablement Benefit (ADD&D). Key details include: No benefit shall be made for any loss resulting from or caused by any of the following:; Insect Bite; Insanity; Pregnancy, childbirth, miscarriage or abortion. Additional details include: Suicide or intentionally self-inflicted injury or any similar attempt whether sane or insane; Travel or flight in or descent from any aircraft, if the insured person is a member of the crew or pilot of the aircraft, or if the flight is made for instructional or training purposes; War or any act incident to war, whether declared or undeclared; Riot, civil commotion or insurrection. Additional details include: Congenital anomalies or conditions arising from there; Any violation or attempt of violation of the law or resistance to arrest; Any kind of disease, sickness or infection other than bacterial infections occurring as a consequence of an accidental cut or wound; Any drug, poison, gas or fumes voluntarily or otherwise taken, administered, absorbed or inhaled, other than as a result of an accident arising from a hazard incident to the member’s occupation. Additional details include: Nuclear weapons or fissionable material, ionizing radiation or contamination by radioactivity from any nuclear fuel or from any nuclear waste, or from the combustion of nuclear fuel, and for the purpose of this exclusion, combustion shall include any self- sustaining process of nuclear fission or fusion; Murder or provoked assault; Any loss in which the proximate cause was the member’s attempt at, or commission of or willful participation in any act or crime punishable under the Revised Penal Code of the Philippines or any other statute except crimes of reckless imprudence as defined in Article 365, or similar laws of any country in which the crime was attempted or committed, or resistance to lawful arrest.

=== SLIDE | 12 | TOTAL & PERMANENT DISABILITY BENEFIT (TPDB) GROUP LIFE INSURANCE ===
If an insured employee becomes totally and permanently disabled for at least six months, Manulife will pay a lump sum equivalent to the Death Benefit to compensate for lost income.

EXCLUSIONS on Total and Permanent Disability Benefit (TPDB). Key details include: No benefit shall be made for any loss resulting from or caused by any of the following:; Any injury intentionally self-inflicted; War or any act incident to war whether declared or undeclared; Riot, civil commotion or insurrection. Additional details include: Travel or flight or descent from any aircraft as a member of the crew, or; Attempted murder; Travel or flight in or descent from any aircraft of any military, naval, or air force; Any violation or attempt of violation of the law or resistance to arrest. Additional details include: Provoked assault, or participation in an assault or brawl; Any drug, poison, gas or fumes, voluntarily or involuntarily taken, administered, absorbed or inhaled, except as a result of an accident arising from a hazard incident to the member’s occupation..

=== SLIDE | 14 | TERMINAL ILLNESS BENEFIT (TIB) GROUP LIFE INSURANCE ===
The Terminal Illness Benefit pays 50% of the basic life coverage in advance if the member is diagnosed with a terminal illness, with the remaining balance paid to the designated beneficiaries.

=== SLIDE | 15 | FAMILY ASSISTANCE BENEFIT (FAB) GROUP LIFE INSURANCE ===
Upon the death of an insured employee, Manulife provides a Family Assistance Benefit to help cover expenses or debts, payable to the same beneficiary as the Life Insurance benefit.
