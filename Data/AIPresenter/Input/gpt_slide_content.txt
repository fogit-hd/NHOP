At FPT Corporation, we understand the importance of a robust social security system in providing peace of mind and financial stability to our employees. Our system offers a range of benefits designed to support you and your loved ones in times of need. For instance, if you experience a permanent disability, whether partial or total, you are entitled to a cash benefit, which could be a monthly pension or a lump sum. This ensures that even in challenging times, you have financial backing. As you approach retirement age and are ready to enjoy the fruits of your labor, a similar benefit structure is in place, offering either a monthly pension or a lump sum. In the unfortunate event of a member's passing, their beneficiaries are supported with a cash benefit, providing essential financial relief during difficult times. Additionally, the funeral benefit assists with burial expenses, easing the financial burden on those who pay for these costs. These benefits reflect our commitment to supporting you throughout your career and beyond, ensuring security and care for you and your family.

Navigating salary loans is made straightforward and efficient with our online system. At FPT, we require all salary loan applications to be processed through the Social Security System's online portal. This digital approach ensures a seamless experience for our employees, allowing you to register and log in at www.sss.gov.ph to submit your loan application. Once submitted, it's important to notify our HR and Finance departments via email to secure the necessary employer approval. Afterward, simply send a screenshot or a photocopy of your loan voucher to our HR department. This step is crucial as it enables us to facilitate your loan payment through salary deduction, making the process hassle-free and integrated into your regular payroll. This streamlined system exemplifies our commitment to providing efficient and supportive services to our team members.
